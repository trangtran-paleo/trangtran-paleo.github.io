# Trang Tran
## Paleoclimatology | Organic Geochemistry

Investigating the drivers of past climate variability using organic geochemical proxies and climate simulations.

---

## About
I am a Ph.D. candidate in Geosciences at the University of Arizona. My research focuses on reconstructing past climate variability using organic geochemical proxies and integrating these records with climate model simulations.

---

## Research

### Hydroclimate Variability
- Leaf-wax δD reconstructions
- Precessional control at tropical margins

- TEX86 and UK'37 proxies
- Indo-Pacific Warm Pool variability
