About Me

Beyond the lab

Hello! This is Trang. I grew up in a small village in Hoa Binh, northwest of Vietnam 🇻🇳. My parents are still living there. Our village is tucked between towering limestone mountains, where rice paddies blanket the lowland valleys. Very beautiful!

My Path to Paleoclimate

I watched climate change unfold in real time — droughts and floods becoming more frequent, each one worse than the last. It felt personal before it felt scientific. Then, in 2014, while doing my first master's degree in Vietnam, I got to meet Dr. Lora Stevens from Cal State Long Beach, who was visiting on a US Fulbright. She introduced me to paleoclimatology — the idea that we don't have a time machine to go back to the past, but we can read Earth's climate history encoded in sediments, fossils, and chemical signatures locked in rock. I was hooked. At that time in Vietnam, this field was pretty innovative, but it felt like exactly the kind of work that mattered — and I knew I wanted to do it.

What keeps me going is a simple but profound problem: human activity has pushed atmospheric CO₂ beyond anything in our short instrumental record. We can't fully understand how Earth will respond just by looking at the last 150+ years. But the geological past? It's been there. It's seen warm worlds, abrupt shifts — from greenhouse to icehouse — and dramatic reorganizations of ocean and atmosphere. That's the archive I work with.

Outside Research

Outside the lab, I love cooking, gardening, and building things. There's something satisfying about growing things slowly and watching them change. During COVID, I built a bunk bed for my daughter — I bought a $5 sketch from Etsy for the dimensions and just went for it. Next on the list: planter boxes.

I'm not a very outdoor person, but my husband is — so we make it work. We enjoy camping and road trips together. And one more thing I'm incredibly proud of — my daughter made it to one of the finalists in 2023 NASA challenge when she was just 7 year old and ended up on NBC LA News! 🎉 She was not a winner but it was such a great journey for her

Outreach & Community

I volunteered at Shark @ The Beach at Cal State Long Beach in 2022 and 2023 — a public outreach event where we taught kids about landslides and earthquakes, explored different landforms and how rain forms, and let them peer at minerals and rocks under microscopes. Seeing kids light up around science is one of my favorite things.
