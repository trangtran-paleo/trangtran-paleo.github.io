Myself

Beyond the lab

I am from Vietnam 🇻🇳. Growing up in a region where environmental change is both visible and immediate shaped how I think about climate — not as an abstract scientific topic, but as something people live with every day. That perspective continues to inform my research and why I care about communicating science clearly.

Background

I became interested in climate science through my experiences growing up in Vietnam. Seeing firsthand how shifting monsoons and flooding affect agriculture, livelihoods, and everyday life made the science feel urgent and personal. This continues to shape how I approach my work and who I hope to reach with it.
