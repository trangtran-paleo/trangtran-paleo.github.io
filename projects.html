Projects

Late Pleistocene Hydroclimate

I reconstruct past precipitation variability using leaf-wax hydrogen isotopes and compare proxy records with climate model simulations.

Sea Surface Temperature Reconstructions

I use organic geochemical proxies, including TEX86 and UK'37, to reconstruct past sea surface temperature variability.

Long-Term Climate Evolution

My ongoing work investigates SST and hydroclimate changes from 10 Ma to 1 Ma.
