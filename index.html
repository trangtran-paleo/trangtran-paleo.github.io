Trang Tran

Paleoclimatology  ·  Paleoceanography  ·  Organic Geochemistry

My research interests lie in paleoclimatology, paleoceanography, and organic geochemistry. I study past climate change using organic geochemical proxies and climate model simulations — investigating how ocean temperatures and hydroclimate respond to elevated atmospheric CO₂ and abrupt climate shifts on geological timescales.
